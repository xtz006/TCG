Why you'll like it

14,000+ cards built in

A complete Chinese card database ships with the app, so search works even with no network.

Scan to identify

Point the camera at the 8-digit password on the lower-left corner — on-device OCR takes you straight to the card.

Full card details

Card types, complete effects, alternate names, and a one-tap copy of the card password.

Always up to date

Check and download the latest card database from Settings in one tap — new cards land in seconds.

Fast, even offline

Card images use a two-level cache, so cards you have viewed open instantly — perfect at the table.

Private by design

No account, no sign-in, no ads, no trackers. Scans and camera frames never leave your device.

How it works

Search

Type a card name or password. The built-in database answers instantly; anything missing falls back to an online search.

Scan

No typing needed. Scan the card's password with the camera or pick a photo from your album.

Read

Get the card's effects and details in Chinese, and copy the password whenever you need it.
